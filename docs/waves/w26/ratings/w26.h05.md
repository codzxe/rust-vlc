## Top-5 defects by shamefulness

1. **Focus ring is invisible in light mode.** `:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid var(--color-accent) }` (`ui/src/a11y/focus.css:16`) renders `#d37918` at 1.41:1 on chrome `#ababab`, 1.82:1 on surface `#c2c2c2`, 1.89:1 on panel `#c6c6c6` — every theme-invariant surface in the family fails WCAG 2.4.11/1.4.11's 3:1. Repro: light `/player`, press Tab. Fix: theme the ring (light needs ≤ ~`#8a4a0a`-class; even `--color-accent-bright` `#b35f0d` only reaches 2.59:1).
2. **The Lead-repinned strip is not delivered.** Seek + volume tracks use `--color-surface` `#c2c2c2` (`ui/src/screens/player/controls/controls.css:283–288`) where `Player - Light.svg` carries its only `#DEDEDE` rect (1238×28 r6 @ (254,1039)) — the scrub affordance reads 1.29:1 vs the frame's 1.71:1, no thumb pixels at rest. Repro: light `/player` idle, sample x400 y870–886. (g05-P1-1 residual.)
3. **Secondary ink fails AA on the surfaces the repin darkened.** `--color-content-secondary` `#4e4e4e` = 3.62:1 on chrome `#ababab` (player timecodes 15px, topbar breadcrumb 11px, onboarding body 14px), 4.29:1 on deep `#bababa` (playlist sidebar labels 11px, count, footer), 4.38:1 on the settings window `#bcbbbc` (inactive nav 15px). The WCAG tuning was done against surface `#c2c2c2` (4.67:1) and never re-run for chrome/deep. Repro: computed styles above; `themes/tokens.json` + per-surface overrides.
4. **Rail and sidebar sit on the wrong side of the repin.** `.app-shell__rail` renders Panel `#c6c6c6` vs frame chrome `#ababab` (ΔRGB 27; `ui/src/components/appshell/appshell.css` rail→Panel mapping imported from the dark frame), and `.lumen-playlist` renders deep `#bababa` vs the frame column's `#c2c2c2`. Repro: light `/player`, sample (26,400) and (150,400). Plus the 215px sidebar vs the frame's 377px.
5. **Chrome↔border collision kills separators and the stepper.** border `#acacac` on chrome `#ababab` is ΔRGB 1: topbar bottom hairline, control-bar top hairline (verified pixels y=30/y=855 on light-player.png), and all six inactive onboarding progress dots (`ui/src/screens/onboarding/onboarding.css` track/dot = `--color-stroke-border`) are optically nonexistent in light. Repro: light `/onboarding`, dots at y≈92–98.

*(Honorable mentions: appdot still `--color-accent` not `--color-accent-icon` (g05-P3-1, `appshell.css`); `--shadow-overlay` 0.7-black under the light menu is a bruise, not a shadow; active rail icon `#c2711a` 2.17:1 on the rail — frame-inherited, still fails.)*

## Would I ship it?

Not as final. The repin was the right call and it *did* land — every surface token renders exactly as pinned, the canvas stays dark with correctly pinned hero inks, and light is now a real theme instead of an inversion accident; playlist/settings are genuinely credible. But shipping a light mode whose keyboard focus ring is invisible, whose front-door body copy sits at 3.62:1, and whose scrub track misses the one value the ramp was repinned around — that's a beta with known nits, not production. Five of my top five are cheap: one focus-ring theme flip, one track token, two ink steps, one border step. Land those and this family is a 7.8+.
