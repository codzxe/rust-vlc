## Would-I-ship summary

**Not as-is — 7.5/10.** The geometry and density engine is genuinely
frame-exact (48px rows, 4px insets, r6/r12, boundary hairlines, 375px app

family with two frames that cannot open, an accelerator ink that has been

hover repin (#1F1F1F), one two-tone icon port, and a 5-line demo-transport
seed. Fix those four and this family is an 8.8+.
