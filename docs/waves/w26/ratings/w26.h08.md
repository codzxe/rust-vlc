## Top-5 defects by shamefulness

1. **global-1: expanding Advanced Options reveals nothing.** Repro: `/settings/global` → click `.settings-advanced__bar`; rows land at win-rel y834/876/922/968 against an 865px window, all `visible:false`, `scrollTop` stays 0. Frame `Settings - global-1.svg` keeps the bar at y645 with all 4 rows visible by y851. Files: `ui/src/screens/settings/areas/index.tsx` (GlobalAreaScreen appends AboutArea), `ui/src/screens/settings/SettingsSections.tsx` (no scroll-into-view on expand). The single worst thing in this family.
2. **About section on global at all.** 113px of `UI package`/`Version` rows at y691 that no global frame contains; it displaced the Advanced bar 141px and directly caused defect 1. Files: `areas/index.tsx`, `areas/updates-about.tsx`. Fix: remove from this screen (or move behind the disclosure it broke).
3. **Ⓘ info affordances missing family-wide: 26 of them.** Frames carry r7.5 `#404040` circles after labels — 6 on global (Startup×2 + Diagnostics×4), 11 on interface, 9 on privacy (verified by SVG circle extraction; y-centers align with row pitch). The app renders zero because the seed has no hint text. Files: `ui/src/screens/settings/SettingField.tsx` (renders `.settings-field__info` only when a hint exists), `schemas/settings.toml` (no `hint` msgids). A systemic row-grammar miss, not a nitpick.
4. **Every frame-affordant action button ships disabled@opacity .5** — Log-file Browse and Skin-file Browse (`SettingField.tsx` path branch) and privacy Clear (`areas/privacy.tsx` ClearHistoryRow). Geometry is frame-exact (100×32 r6 `#191919` @x924); state is not. Honest-inert is the right *pattern*, but three dead buttons against zero live ones on three screens is a visible capability vacuum.
5. **`--color-content-tertiary` = `#828282` vs frame `#6B6B6B`** on sidebar group labels, segmented inactive text, switch OFF text (g10-P3-2, one-token fix, still open — token lane: `themes/tokens.json`). Dishonorable mentions: language value "English (United States)" vs frame "English"; theme segmented selected cell is #2 (Dark) where the frame selects cell #1 (with Figma stub labels "1/2/3" — real Light/Dark/System labels are the sane call, but the default selection position deviates).

## Would I ship this?

Not the global screen pair — the About insertion and the expanding-into-void disclosure are ship-blockers, full stop. Interface and privacy I would ship tomorrow with a punch list: the missing ⓘ grammar (26 icons), the disabled Browse/Clear row, and the tertiary token. The skin itself — shells, rows, switches, dropdowns, sliders, swatches — is measured frame-exact to a degree I rarely see (3px switch gaps matched against SVG path bboxes), light mode is coherent, zero console errors, nothing clips at 1024×768, and keyboard/focus behavior is genuinely good (initial focus on the active nav item, 2px `#D37918` rings, aria-valuetext on sliders). This family is one honest content-mapping pass away from YES; today it's a 7.9 that earns every decimal of the missing 2.1.
