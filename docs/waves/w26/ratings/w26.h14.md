## Top-5 defects by shamefulness

1. **Light theme erases the recessed-well grammar and fails contrast.** Tracks #c2c2c2 on cards #c6c6c6 (Δ4/255), collapsed pills Δ4 on the dialog body, viz trigger ring Δ4 — the frames define wells at Δ16 inversion; and label/value ink #5f5f5f on #c6c6c6 is **3.76:1, under the 4.5:1 AA floor for 15px text**. Files: `tools/dynamics/dynamics.css` (`.lumen-slider__track`, `.dynamics-tool__toggle`), `tools/visualizer/visualizer.css` (`.viz-select__trigger`, `.viz-display`); root cause `themes/tokens.json` light ramp (token lane; cross-lane with EQ). Repro: `12_light_compressor.png`, probe-light.json.
2. **Thumb overhangs the fill end 3.5–5.6px on all 16 dynamics rows; frames paint flush 16/16.** `components/slider/slider.css` `left: calc((100% - 8px) * p/100)` vs fill `left 2px; width calc((100% - 4px) * p/100)` — flush needs thumb left `calc((100% - 4px) * p/100 - 6px)`. Visible at 100% zoom on every row (5.6px = 70% of the thumb's 8px width past the fill). Repro: `02/03/04` shots, row 1. Cross-lane with EQ/video (same primitive).
3. **Dynamics chevrons ~30% undersized with a 4–5px inset miss** — painted 4×8 / 8×4 @ stroke ~1.0 (Icon default 16) vs frame `Vector 27/27_2` ≈6.5×11.5 / 11.5×6.5 @ sw1.5 @ 19px inset. The frame-exact fix (Icon `size={24}`) already ships in this family's own `VisualizerTool.tsx:80` — three cards away, unexamined, for two waves. Files: `tools/dynamics/DynamicsCard.tsx:81`, `dynamics.css`.
4. **Visualizer dropdown opens 164px below the dialog fold** — 70% of the 232px list hidden behind the dialog body's scroll clip (measured: list bottom 872.25 vs body bottom 708.75); no flip-up, no scroll-into-view on open; pointer users pick from a list they cannot see. Files: `tools/visualizer/visualizer.css` (`.viz-select__list`), `VisualizerModeSelect.tsx`. Repro: `06_visualizer_dropdown_open.png`.
5. **`RESTING_LEVELS.spectrum` bar-4 typo (19 vs frame 16) + the #828282-vs-#6B6B6B ink band (Δ23).** The resting display misstates the frame's proportions on 5 of 6 bars (`types.ts:93`); the ink band is the same unresolved token ask the EQ rater logged last lane (g15-P2-1) — two families, two waves, one missing `content-quiet` step. Files: `tools/visualizer/types.ts`, `dynamics.css`.

## Would I ship it

**No.** Dark mode is data-perfect (labels, values, units, tick counts, pitch — all frame-verbatim, and I mean every string) and two afternoons from shippable: fix the thumb formula, copy the viz lane's `size={24}` chevron, correct one number in `types.ts`, and decide the dropdown's fold behavior. What blocks shipping is that the family's polish debts have now survived two fix waves untouched while the same three one-liners got re-documented — plus a light theme where the controls' defining surface grammar is invisible and the text fails AA. The visualizer dropdown is the one piece I'd sign off today, fold bug aside. Family mean **7.6** — good bones, unshipped discipline.
