## Would I ship it?

Not as-is. Crop and Essential are genuinely close — frame-exact inner grids, the best slider anatomy in the program, flawless light mode and keyboard — and I'd ship either behind one decision on card width. Colors needs one afternoon of margin math (the compression is six scoped rules in `video.css`). HDR I would not ship at all in its frame state: a dropdown whose five of six options are painted outside its clipping container, with clicks falling through to the scrim, is a functional defect masquerading as a styling one — and the same shared primitive will bite every dialog-hosted dropdown in the product until the Dropdown learns about scrollports, not viewports.
