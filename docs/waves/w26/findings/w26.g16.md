### [g16-P2-1] Dynamics card chevron glyph renders ~20% small and 5px shy of the frame's right inset

- **State/repro:** /player → gear (or Ctrl+E) → Audio tab. Collapsed card:
  painted chevron-› measures **5×8px** (scan of `01_tools_audio_collapsed.png`,
  x891–895/y425–432); frame `Vector 27` = **5×10, sw 1.5** (x356–361, y39–49).
  Expanded header: painted chevron-∨ **8×5** (`02_compressor_expanded.png`,
  x890–897/y276–280); frame `Vector 27_2` = **10×5, sw 1.5**. Stroke renders
  ~1.0 (16px Icon box scales 1.5→1.0) vs frame 1.5. Glyph right inset 24px vs
  frame 19px (card right edge minus stroke bbox).
- **Files:** `ui/src/screens/player/tools/dynamics/DynamicsCard.tsx` (Icon at
  default `size=16`), `ui/src/screens/player/tools/dynamics/dynamics.css`
  (`.dynamics-tool__toggle` padding / no chevron sizing).
- **Frame evidence:** `design/figma/Audio - Compressor Settings.svg` ids
  `Vector 27` / `Vector 27_2` (identical in the Spatializer/Widener frames).
- **Fix direction:** adopt the visualizer lane's treatment — `Icon size={24}`
  (paints 5×10/10×5 at sw 1.5, verified exact on `.viz-tool__chevron`) with a
  calibrated right inset (≈ −8px margin or padding-right ≈25px) so the stroke
  lands 19px off the card edge. Affects all three dynamics cards.

### [g16-P2-2] Light mode: the slider track well, collapsed toggle card and visualizer trigger ring are invisible (Δ1/255 contrast)

- **State/repro:** /player → toggle `document.documentElement.dataset.theme
  = "light"` → open Tools → Audio. Measured (`12_light_compressor.png` +
  computed styles): slider track `--color-surface` **#f0f0f0** on card
  `--color-surface-panel` **#efefef** — the 366×16 track is indistinguishable
  from the card (pixel-sampled: track rgb(240,240,240) vs panel
  rgb(239,239,239)). Same math collapses the collapsed 48px toggle card
  (surface on the dialog's panel fill) and the visualizer's 2px trigger ring
  (`viz-select__trigger` surface vs `viz-select__field` panel). Dark mode is
  unaffected (Δ16).
- **Files:** consumers `ui/src/screens/player/tools/dynamics/dynamics.css`
  (`.lumen-slider__track`, `.dynamics-tool__toggle`), `ui/src/screens/player/
  tools/visualizer/visualizer.css` (`.viz-select__trigger`, `.viz-display`);
  root cause is the light ramp in `themes/tokens.json` (token lane) — light
  `surface` #f0f0f0 vs `surface-panel` #efefef leaves no recessed-well step.
- **Frame evidence:** frames pin the dark well `#090909` on `#1B1B1B`
  (Δ16); no light frames exist — contract §7 "dark + light both coherent".
- **Fix direction:** systemic: either the token lane re-steps the light
  surface ramp, or wells map to a token that stays recessed in both themes
  (`--color-surface-deep` #dedede in light, as `slider.css` already does for
  seekbar/volume wells). **Cross-lane:** `equalizer.css`/`slider.css`
  `.lumen-eq-slider` and `dialogs.css` `.tools-row__value` carry the same
  `--color-surface` well mapping — fix once, everywhere.
