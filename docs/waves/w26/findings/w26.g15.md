## Findings

### [g15-P1-1] EQ fader rail overflows the tools dialog — 6 of 11 faders unreachable in view
- **State + reproduce**: `/player` → Ctrl+E → Audio tab → click "Open Equalizer". The `.eq-panel__rail` is fixed `width: 748px` inside a 398px panel, and `.eq-panel__body` sets no clip, so the rail runs to x1287 while the dialog surface ends at x940 — **347px of fader columns sit under the scrim** (`elementFromPoint` at the 12 KHz column → `lumen-scrim`). Only 5 columns fit fully; the 1 KHz column is sliced mid-chip at the dialog edge (§7 "no clipped text"), and 3K/6K/12K/14K/16 K are invisible. The rail's own pan never engages (`scrollWidth == clientWidth == 748`, `scrollLeft` immovable); instead the **dialog body** becomes the scroller (`overflow-y:auto` computes `overflow-x:auto`): scrollWidth 786 vs clientWidth 438, pans to 348 shifting the whole dialog content. The w26.f05 design ("mounts narrower than the 784px frame pan the rail horizontally") is not delivered.
- **Responsible files**: `ui/src/screens/player/tools/equalizer/equalizer.css` (`.eq-panel__rail`, w26.f05 lane); compounding context: `dialogs/ToolsDialogShell.tsx` body slot (w26.a18) has no width guard.
- **Frame evidence**: `EQ.svg` Variant2 — one 784px panel, 11 columns visible at 68px pitch; `report2.json` `overflowProof` (rail 539→1287 vs surface right 940; `dialogBodyScrolled` 786/438/348; `elementAt6K` = `lumen-scrim`; `colsInsideSurface: 5`).
- **Fix direction**: make the rail the scrollport, not the fixed frame: `.eq-panel__rail { width: 100% }` (drop the 748px width; columns stay `flex: none` at 68) keeping `overflow-x: auto` + `scrollbar-width: thin` — rail then pans internally (748 content in a 362 viewport), the dialog body returns to 438 scrollWidth, every column keeps frame geometry. Verify the thumb's 4px overhangs never clip at pan extremes.

### [g15-P2-1] Band label ink #828282 instead of frame #6B6B6B (all 11 labels)
- **State + reproduce**: EQ expanded (any state). Measured label color `rgb(130,130,130)` on every `Preamp`/`60 Hz`…`16 KHz` label.
- **Responsible files**: `ui/src/screens/player/tools/equalizer/equalizer.css` (`.eq-panel__label` uses `var(--color-content-tertiary)` with a comment claiming "frame: #6B6B6B"); the token is pinned `#828282` in `ui/src/tokens/generated/tokens.css` ← `themes/tokens.json` (token lane).
- **Frame evidence**: `EQ.svg` label paths `#Preamp`/`#60 Hz`… fill `#6B6B6B` (also `Audio - Equalizer.svg` row labels); measured `rgb(130,130,130)` = `#828282` — Δ23/255 per channel, visibly brighter than the frame's quiet ink. `report.json` `expanded.columns[*].labelStyle.color`.
- **Fix direction**: token-lane handoff — pin the frame `#6B6B6B` (repin `--color-content-tertiary` is risky for other consumers; cleanest is a quiet-ink token) — raw hex can't land in equalizer.css (stylelint semantic-tokens-only). Until then this stays open.
