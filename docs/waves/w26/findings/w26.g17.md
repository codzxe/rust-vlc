## Findings

### [g17-P1-1] HDR dropdown list is clipped by the tools dialog — 5 of 6 options invisible and unclickable at open
- **State + reproduce**: `/player` @1440×900 → Ctrl+E → **Video** tab → click the HDR trigger (cards collapsed). The list opens downward from the LAST section in the dialog: `Auto` (y596.8–644.8) is the only option inside the dialog body (bottom 645.25); `Off/HDR10/HDR10+/Dolby Vision/HLG` (y644.8–884.8) paint below the clip. Hit-test probe: `document.elementFromPoint` on options 2–6 returns **`.lumen-scrim`** — mouse clicks fall through; reaching them requires scrolling the tools-dialog body or keyboard arrows (focus auto-scrolls, verified: body scrollTop jumps to 243, HLG lands at y596.8).
- **Frame evidence**: `HDR_Options.png`/`HDR Options.svg` `state=open` — the full 362×278 list with all six 44px rows visible below the trigger.
- **Responsible**: shared primitive `ui/src/components/menu/Dropdown.tsx` (list is `position:absolute; top:calc(100%+4px)` with **no collision/flip/scrollport logic**) + mounting context `ui/src/screens/player/dialogs/ToolsDialogShell.tsx` and `dialogs.css` (`.player-dialog__body { overflow-y:auto }`, `.lumen-dialog { overflow:hidden }`); lane mount `ui/src/screens/player/tools/video/HdrTool.tsx`.
- **Fix direction**: scrollport-aware Dropdown (flip up when the list would cross the nearest scroll container's bottom, or `max-height` + internal scroll), or portal the list out of the dialog scroll container. Same mechanism will clip the **Gradient sub-dropdown** (Colors card) whenever its row sits near the body's bottom after scrolling [VERIFY], and likely the audio tab's EQ presets dropdown — Lead should route one shared fix.

### [g17-P2-1] Colors card vertical rhythm compressed — lower-half rows sit 11–26px higher than the frame
- **State + reproduce**: Video tab → Colors expanded; measure row y-offsets from the card top against the SVG's group bboxes.
- **Frame evidence vs app** (offsets from card top): hex1 54 vs 59; sep1 106 vs 104; hex2 126 vs 118; Saturation label 177.5 vs 162, track 206 vs 194; Similarity 253.5/282 vs 238/270; sep2 326 vs 306; **Negate 346 vs 320 (−26)**; sep3 388 vs 365; Posterize 398 vs 379; sep4 440 vs 424; Gradient 455 vs 438; sub-dropdown 502 vs 485; Color 565 vs 548; Cartoon 612 vs 595; sep5 659 vs 640; Sepia label 682.5 vs 668, track 711 vs 700. Card content (sans the extra staged note) ends 738.5 vs frame 755. Root cause: `video.css` uses a **uniform 13px/13px separator rhythm** while the frame varies 20/20 (hex section), 20/20 (post-Similarity), 10/10, 10/15, 15/23.5; plus the 12px hex→slider seam (frame 19.5) and the 16px slider-end→sep margin (frame 20). The Gradient→Color→Cartoon block (15/47px pitches) is frame-exact.
- **Responsible**: `ui/src/screens/player/tools/video/video.css` (`.video-sep` margins, `.video-row + .dynamics-row`, `.dynamics-row + .video-sep`).
- **Fix direction**: replace the uniform sep margins with per-gap frame values (20/20, 20/20, 10/10, 10/15, 15/23.5), hex2 seam 19.5px, slider-end margin 20px — ~6 scoped margin rules.
