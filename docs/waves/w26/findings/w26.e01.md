## Findings

### F1 — P1 · Appearance step · selected-accent ring never renders (CSS selector ↔ DOM attribute mismatch)
- **Evidence:** `onboarding.css:539` keys the ring on `.onboarding__accent[aria-pressed="true"]::after`,
  but the accent buttons are `role="radio"` with **`aria-checked`** (`OnboardingScreen.tsx:734-735`) —
  `aria-pressed` is never set, so the selector never matches. Computed `::after` on the checked
  radio: `border-width: 0px`, `inset: auto` (report.json `accentRing`). Pixel samples at the ring
  position (12 px left of the selected orange dot, and 30 px above it) in `win-appearance.png`:
  pure `#161616` window bg — no ring. Frame `Onboarding-1.svg` "Frame 153":
  `<rect x="43" y="657" width="60" height="60" rx="30" stroke="#D37918" stroke-opacity="0.3"
  stroke-width="4"/>` around the selected dot. Screenshot: `/tmp/shots-w26.e01/win-appearance.png`
  [VERIFY — deterministic DOM+pixel proof; vision 429].
- **Location:** `ui/src/screens/onboarding/onboarding.css:539` (selector), `OnboardingScreen.tsx:734-741` (radio semantics — correct as-is).
- **Suggested fix:** change the selector to `.onboarding__accent[aria-checked="true"]::after` (radio semantics stay). Add a test asserting the selected dot carries the ring state (e.g. a class or computed `::after`).
- **Confidence:** high.

### F2 — P2 · Appearance step · frame's nested preview panel (window-in-window) is absent
- **Evidence:** frames `Onboarding-1/-3..10` compose the step as window `#0E0E0E` containing an
  inner **1056×594 `#090909` panel** (inset ≈17 px) with a 1056×22 `#191919` title bar and a
  mini seekbar strip (`#371F06` + `#D37918`) at its bottom — a nested player preview; heading sits
  at its top (y185-208), cards mid-panel; dot row + buttons sit on the `#0E0E0E` chrome below.
  The app renders one flat `#161616` window with the step vertically centered — no inner panel,
  title bar or seekbar strip. Render scanline y=150: `#090909` x14-885; app window computed bg
  uniform `rgb(22,22,22)`. Block-grid: 33/600 blocks >28 concentrated in the card/dot bands.
  Screenshot: `/tmp/shots-w26.e01/step-03-appearance.png` [VERIFY].
- **Location:** `ui/src/screens/onboarding/onboarding.css:54-79` (`.onboarding__window`), `OnboardingScreen.tsx:605-625`.
- **Suggested fix:** either add the recessed preview well (inner panel on `--color-surface-deep` + hairline title bar) behind appearance content, or have the Lead record this as a sanctioned recomposition. Spec §2 (line 100) describes only "centered hero … #161616 frame" — frames carry more anatomy than the spec text; per spec §0 the frames are source of truth.
- **Confidence:** high on the delta; medium on intent (code comments cite a "wave plan" that is not in the repo).
