### [g04-P1-2] Play-pill hover is a double miss in dark: fill doesn't change AND the glyph flips bright
- **State/reproduce**: open media, pause, hover the play pill → measured pill bg `rgb(211,121,24)` (unchanged #D37918) and glyph pixels `#EAEAEA` on orange.
- **Files**: `ui/src/screens/player/controls/controls.css:117-119` (hover → `var(--color-accent-bright)`); `ui/src/tokens/generated/tokens.css:32` (`--color-accent-bright: #d37918` — identical to `--color-accent` in dark); `ui/src/components/icon/icon.css:23-25` (`.lumen-icon-host:hover .lumen-icon { color: var(--color-content-bright) }` sets `color` directly and beats the pill's `--color-stroke-icon` remap, which only feeds `.lumen-icon`'s own base rule).
- **Frame evidence**: CONTRACT §2 pins hover `#e8851a`; `Button.svg`/play.svg keep the glyph dark at every state (button.css restates the on-accent remap under `:hover/:active/:focus-visible` at equal specificity — controls.css does not).
- **Fix**: (a) token lane: dark `accent.bright` must be #E8851A, not a copy of the primary (light already diverges to #B35F0D); (b) controls.css: restate `--color-stroke-icon: var(--color-content-on-accent)` under `.lumen-controls__play:hover/:active/:focus-visible` (button.css primary pattern, incl. the `:root .lumen-player` (0,4,0) companions).
- **Confidence**: HIGH (computed style + pixels).
