## Findings

### [g11-P1-1] Privacy: "Clear history now" row + Clear button missing entirely
- **Screen/reproduce**: /settings/privacy, default state. Frame History section has a
  4th row — label "Clear history now" + Small-action control **Clear** (100×32 rx6
  #191919, label #8F8F8F, @ x924 y244 — SVG rect extracted). Live renders only 3
  History rows; no DOM node, no schema key, nothing in its place.
- **Responsible**: `schemas/settings.toml:331-335` (w26.f01 comment defers the action
  row to "the privacy-area composition lane" — but no such lane exists: privacy is
  pure `GENERATED_SCREENS.privacy`, `areas/index.tsx:167-190`); needs a hand-authored
  privacy area composition (pattern: `areas/controls.tsx` ControlsArea embedding
  GENERATED_SCREENS) or an `action` row type in the field grammar
  (`SettingField.tsx`/`SettingsSections.tsx`, w26.f02 field lane).
- **Frame evidence**: `Settings - privacy.svg` rect `x=924 y=244 w=100 h=32 rx=6
  fill=#191919`; a20 §C3 "Clear history now → Clear button 100×32".
- **Fix direction**: hand-author PrivacyArea (compose GENERATED_SCREENS.privacy + the
  action row after the history section), or extend the schema with an action entry
  rendered by SettingField as a `Small action` 100×32 rx6 #191919 control. Real
  behavior: clears recent-items history (client seam).
- **Confidence**: HIGH (SVG rect + DOM absence both measured).

### [g11-P2-1] Privacy: extra "Data & privacy" section (4 rows) not in the frame
- **Screen/reproduce**: /settings/privacy. Live renders section "Data & privacy"
  (telemetry toggle, crash-reports dropdown, usage-stats toggle, update-checks toggle)
  BEFORE History. The frame carries no such section anywhere on privacy (a20 §C3;
  SVG rect inventory: History + Network controls only); the frame family places
  "Check for updates automatically" in **global** Startup (a20 §C1) and telemetry
  rows nowhere. Consequence: History lands at y395 (frame y83) and the column
  overflows (scrollH 830 > clientH 787) where the frame fits unscrolled.
- **Responsible**: `schemas/settings.toml:284-329` (w26.f01 seed, "pinned telemetry
  data section") + `ui/src/screens/settings/generated/Screens.tsx` (PrivacyScreen
  renders the seeded section). Merge owner: Lead + schema lane (a20 D3 marked
  privacy NEEDS-MERGE).
- **Frame evidence**: `Settings - privacy.svg` — 29 content rects, zero rows above
  History y114 other than the 3 History + 5 Network + Clear; a20 §C3.
- **Fix direction**: Lead decision — (a) fold the 4 rows behind privacy's in-flow
  Advanced disclosure (grammar exists, SettingsSections), or (b) relocate
  update-checks → global Startup per frame and telemetry rows → diagnostics/global;
  then re-emit the seed. Do NOT drop keys silently (registry guard).
- **Confidence**: HIGH.
