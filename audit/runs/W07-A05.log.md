### Is sample-rate conversion / resampling correct where it exists?

No SRC exists in the shipped audio path — verified:
- The audio-engine's only resampler is `MicroResampler` (`crates/audio-engine/src/resample.rs`, W07-A02's file), a ±1000 ppm DRIFT corrector fed `DriftCorrection` values (pipeline.rs:159-164, 348-363) — not a sample-rate converter.
- The null device opens at the DECODED stream's own rate (`crates/audio-output/src/null.rs:80-86`, `SampleCounter::new(format.rate)`) — every decoder's native rate is accepted as-is; nothing anywhere converts rates.
- Rate plumbing in my files is identity-through: pcm/alac/adpcm/opus emit the stream's own declared rate in `AudioFormat` (facts/cookie/head). Opus's RFC 7845 fixed 48 kHz output is handled by DECLARATION (`opus/head.rs` DECODE_RATE), not resampling.
- The one rate defect found is my S2 (mp2/decoder.rs F-001): the contract decoder latches `out_rate` at the FIRST frame; a mid-stream rate change silently keeps the stale rate in every later `AudioFrame` (pts base + duration wrong) — and since no resampler exists downstream, the wrong rate flows straight into the device clock. Same staleness applies to channel count (stale-sample mixing / truncation).
