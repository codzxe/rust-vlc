## 1. One-paragraph verdict
The most historically load-bearing fixture in the ASF family: it is the exact file whose sha assert killed CI run 43373340224 (2026-11-13) — the ffmpeg-6.1.x-on-runners byte drift on **wmav2**, a lossy encoder, made the regenerated bytes fail the 7.1.5-measured pin — which is the documented reason the whole family was committed in-tree (ASF-d, Lead commit 8572a8e5).
