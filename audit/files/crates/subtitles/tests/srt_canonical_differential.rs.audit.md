## 1. One-paragraph verdict
The assertions are exact, byte-strong, and I independently verified every asserted id/µs/text
against the committed fixture bytes (all match, including the 24 h rollover 86,399,900,000 →
86,400,100,000 µs and the UTF-16 variants). What I cannot verify is the header's provenance
claim: the fixtures are asserted to be ffmpeg 7.1.5 `-c:s srt` output generated from
"truth1/2/3.vtt" inputs, but no truth VTTs, no generation script, and no checksums are
committed — so the "differential vs ffmpeg" and "no echo chamber (D-9)" claims rest on an
unreproducible generation step. The fixture CONTENT is ffmpeg-plausible (kept `<b>/<i>/<u>`,
dropped `<font color>` — matching the header's own note), so this is REAL-UNPROVEN provenance
wrapped around a REAL test, not a fabricated oracle.
