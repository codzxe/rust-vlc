## Test design

The suite proves the five required properties at the two deepest
landed layers — the real engine seam (`FrameTap`) and the instrumented
twin (`CanvasBridge`+`MailboxTransport`, the only landed layer that
still exposes full `MailboxStats`) — and, for the acceptance, the
whole GUI funnel:

1. **`fx6_a04_frames_produced_vs_dropped_at_the_mailbox`** — a 60 fps
   producer against a 20 Hz consumer (pipeline.rs's stressor cadences
   on the virtual Timeline): occupancy asserted ≤ capacity after EVERY
   offer; final counters exact: `offered == 60` (produced N), the
   harness's own drain count == `stats.drawn` (the counter mirrors
   reality), conservation exact (`offered == drawn + dropped_overflow +
   dropped_stale` on the emptied mailbox), `dropped_overflow > 0`,
   `drawn < offered` (shipped ≤ produced), and the two produced
   counters agree (`BridgeStats.frames_shipped == offered`).
2. **`fx6_a04_drop_policy_honored_on_overflow`** — the real seam
   (`FrameTap`, production capacity 2): 7 marked frames offered, no
   drain; exactly 2 records survive; the survivors are the NEWEST two
   proven on CONTENT (frame ids 5/6 AND the marker pixel each payload
   carries — not just headers claiming to be), PTS-ascending,
   `frames_shipped == 7` (the producer was never blocked).
3. **`fx6_a04_timestamps_pass_through_the_lane`** — (a) the real
   seam at 1:1 pace: a frame pushed at stamp T drains/decodes with
   `pts_ns == T` exactly, emit stamps monotonic; (b) the instrumented
   twin on the virtual Timeline: BOTH stamps exact — `pts_ns == T` and
   `emitted_ns == the known clock reading` (no fabrication; the stamps
   survive the wire codec's encode→decode round trip inside the
   transport).
4. **`fx6_a04_stalled_consumer_is_bounded`** — the W14-A07 class at
   the landed seam: 512 offers with the consumer NEVER draining. Both
   halves: the `FrameTap` (every offer Ok — never blocks the pump;
   `frames_shipped == 512`; one drain ships exactly the capacity) and
   the twin (occupancy == `min(offered, capacity)` per step — the
   bound is tight; `dropped_overflow == 512 − capacity` exact; the
   backlog-inclusive conservation identity mid-stall — honoring
   mailbox.rs audit F-002's empty-queue precondition for
   `conserves()` — then the end-of-stall drain closes `conserves()`
   exactly).
5. **`fx6_a04_frame_counter_matches_decode_count_at_normal_pace`** —
   the acceptance, two halves. (a) The whole GUI funnel
   (`ShellRuntime::open_path` → `PlaybackPlay` through the single
   command → ONLY `pump_once` ticks → the per-tick frame drain, the
   ticker's exact body) drives the real AVI fixture to `Ended`
   (position 2_000_000 µs), with the instrumented tap riding the same
   injector the shell's bind uses. The counters: the decode-count
   oracle re-derived through the GUI funnel (`scheduler_pushed == 60`,
   `dropped_late == 0`, `frames_accepted == 120` — pipeline_accounting's
   pinned numbers); `offered == video_presented` (the tap mirrors every
   renderer present — the engine's own counter); `offered ≥ 60`
   (produced covers every decoded frame); conservation exact; every
   shipped record counted (`records.len() == drawn` — a drain-side
   skip, the frame_tap.rs audit F-002 gap, would show here), each one
   decodable LUF1, PTS in media order. The whole-ring `pump_once`
   caveat (its per-pump bursts can overflow the capacity-2 mailbox by
   policy) is documented in the test: the identity pinned is
   conservation + the counter-vs-decode-count mirror, not a zero-drop
   pin. (b) The zero-overflow steady state at the lane's normal pace —
   a 30 fps producer against a 60 Hz consumer on the instrumented
   twin: `offered == drawn == 60`, `dropped_overflow == 0`, occupancy
   bounded per offer. The production ticker's own zero-overflow drive
   (`pump_session`, the wall-bounded pump) has no injectable-clock
   seam (shell.rs audit F-004) — a src change this tests-only
   assignment must not make; (b) is the deepest in-process proof of
   that state.

## Risks & followups

1. **The tests have not executed on this box** (cargo forbidden; the
   orchestrator compiles centrally). They are calibrated against
   proven neighbors: the shell.rs pacer proofs (same runtime, same
   pump loop, same 1024-pump bound — passing in the baseline), FX4-A03
   (same funnel/pump shape), `tests/pipeline.rs` (the same twin-lane
   harness), `frame_tap.rs`'s own unit tests (the same FrameTap
   drives), and pipeline_accounting's exact oracles for the same
   fixture. If a bound or an exact number is off, the assert fails
   loudly — a calibration fix, not a design change.
2. **`stats.offered == accounting.video_presented`** assumes the tap
   mirrors every successful renderer present — read-verified at
   `pipeline_host.rs:1066-1078` (LatchingPresenter) and `:1145-1158`
   (render_request): both tap after the renderer succeeds, and
   `video.presented` counts the same deliveries. A present-path error
   mid-run breaks the assert loudly — which is the test's job.
3. **The drain-side skip observability gap** (frame_tap.rs audit
   F-002: a skipped drain-side encode is counted nowhere): my
   `records.len() == stats.drawn` assert makes the skip VISIBLE in
   this drive (records under drawn). The durable fix (a
   `drain_skipped` counter) belongs to the mailbox/tap owners.
4. **`pump_session` (the production wall-bounded ticker pump) remains
   untested** (shell.rs audit F-004, doubled by my report): the
   zero-overflow state is proven at the lane level (test 5(b)), not on
   the production pump; the missing piece is an injectable-clock seam
   — a deliberate src change left for the owner (not made: tests-only
   assignment).
5. **The GUI-side counter surfacing is FX6-A02's and FX6-A05's lane**
   (the tauri ticker's counters; MediaSurface's canvas counter): my
   tests pin the Rust lane that feeds it. Until A02 lands, the
   `records.last()` ship on the tauri side still silently drops all
   but the newest record per tick — the mailbox drops counted here do
   not reach the webview as records at all.
